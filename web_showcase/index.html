Zero-cloud offline setup guide

Set up in 2 minutes before your lecture begins.

1

Turn on Mobile Hotspot (No Internet Needed)

Enable your phone’s Mobile Hotspot. Connect your Windows laptop to this hotspot. Mobile data can remain turned off; the devices communicate over direct local IPv4.

2

Tap P2P Sync in WorkshopFlow

Open WorkshopFlow on Windows and tap the Sync Hub icon in the app bar. The local sync server will display an IP like http://192.168.43.1:8089.

3

Automatic Real-Time Sync

Any audio recorded on your phone or slide snapped during the workshop instantly streams to your laptop’s SQLite database.

1

Download Qwen 2.5 on LM Studio

Open LM Studio on your Windows laptop and download qwen2.5-7b-instruct (or 3B for lightweight laptops).

2

Start Local Server on Port 1234

Navigate to the Local Server tab (`<->`), choose your loaded model, and click Start Server.

3

1-Tap Translation & Synthesis

Inside WorkshopFlow, select "Auto-Detect & Translate to English (Tamil / Tanglish)" and tap Translate & Generate.

1

Snap Whiteboards with 1-Tap

When the speaker writes formulas on the board or flips slides, point your phone and tap the snap button. It automatically attaches to the exact second in the audio stream.

2

Pin Questions & Aha! Moments

Tap "Mark Question" or "Important" anytime during the talk. Later, click that question on your laptop to jump the audio directly to that explanation.
